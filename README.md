# Sales Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales and profit data through interactive dashboards and visualizations. The objective is to identify sales trends, compare regional performance, evaluate product category performance, and communicate actionable business insights using data storytelling techniques.

## Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit across product sub-categories.
- Identify monthly sales trends and fluctuations.
- Compare profitability across different regions.
- Evaluate sales performance by product category.
- Present business insights through clear and effective visualizations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau** — Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** — Data storage and preparation

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Sub-category
A horizontal bar chart compares profit across product sub-categories, helping identify the most profitable products and categories with potential losses.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart displays monthly sales fluctuations to highlight sales peaks, declines, and potential seasonal patterns.

### 3. Sales by Region
A bar chart compares profit across the Central, East, South, and West regions to identify strong-performing and underperforming regions.

### 4. Sales by Category
A horizontal bar chart compares total sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

## Key Findings

- **Technology** records the highest sales at approximately 839,893.
- **Furniture** generates sales of approximately 754,748.
- **Office Supplies** records sales of approximately 731,893.
- The **West region** has the highest profit, followed by the East, South, and Central regions.
- **Copiers** are among the strongest profit-generating sub-categories shown in the dashboard.
- **Bookcases** show negative profit, indicating a need to investigate costs, pricing, and discount strategies.
- Monthly sales fluctuate, with visible peaks in September and November.

*Note: Values and observations are based on the displayed dashboard. Further analysis of the underlying dataset is needed to validate business causes and seasonal patterns.*

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate losses in Bookcases and review discounting, pricing, and operational costs.
- Analyze the factors contributing to strong Copier profitability.
- Study the West region's performance to identify strategies that may benefit other regions.
- Monitor monthly sales fluctuations to support inventory and sales planning.
- Focus on category-level performance to identify opportunities for sustainable growth.
