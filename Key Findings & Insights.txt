Key Findings & Insights

- Sales by Category: Technology leads with total sales of 839,893, followed by Furniture (754,748) and Office Supplies (731,893).
- Sales by Region: The West region records the highest profit (approximately 110K), followed by East (95K), South (47K), and Central (40K).
- Sales by Sub-category: Copiers generate the highest profit among the displayed sub-categories, while Bookcases show a negative profit, indicating a potential area for improvement.
- Monthly Sales Trend: Sales fluctuate throughout the year, with noticeable peaks in September and November and a dip in October.
- Business Recommendation: Focus on high-performing categories and profitable sub-categories, investigate losses from Bookcases, and analyze monthly sales fluctuations to improve overall profitability.
